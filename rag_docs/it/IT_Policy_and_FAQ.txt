1. IT Support Process
=====================

The IT Service Desk is the single entry point for all hardware, software,
network, and access issues. Employees must NOT contact IT engineers
directly via Slack, WhatsApp, or personal phone. All requests must be
raised through a ticket so they can be tracked, prioritised, and audited.

How to raise a ticket
---------------------
- Use the AI Copilot ("I have a problem with X") and it will create the
  ticket on your behalf with the correct category and priority.
- Alternatively, email itsupport@novigosolutions.com with the subject line
  "[<category>] <one-line problem>" — a ticket is created automatically.
- For severe outages affecting more than ten users, also page the on-call
  IT engineer at +91-80-IT-ON-CALL.

Service Level Agreements
------------------------
Response and resolution times are tracked from the moment the ticket
status moves to "open":

- Critical: response within 30 minutes, resolution target 2 hours.
  Examples: production system outage, mass email failure, VPN down for
  the entire office, ransomware suspicion.
- High: response within 1 hour, resolution target 4 hours.
  Examples: an individual cannot log in, business-critical software
  crash, hardware failure for a senior leader.
- Medium: response within 4 hours, resolution target 1 business day.
  Examples: laptop slowness, Outlook sync issue, peripheral not
  detected, software install request for an approved tool.
- Low: response within 1 business day, resolution target 3 business
  days. Examples: cosmetic issues, training questions, low-impact
  configuration changes.

Out-of-hours support
--------------------
The Service Desk operates 09:00–19:00 IST on business days. For
critical issues outside these hours, page the on-call engineer.
Medium and low priority tickets raised after hours will be picked up the
next business day.


2. Asset Request Policy
========================

Eligibility
-----------
Every full-time employee is entitled to one company laptop, one
docking station, and up to two external monitors as part of standard
provisioning. Anything beyond the standard kit must be requested
through an asset request and is subject to manager approval.

Standard hardware refresh cycle
-------------------------------
- Laptops: refreshed every 3 years from the date of joining (or device
  assignment, whichever is later).
- Monitors and peripherals: refreshed only on failure or after 5 years.
- Headsets: refreshed every 2 years for client-facing roles, otherwise
  on failure.

How to request an asset
-----------------------
Submit a request via the AI Copilot ("I need a second monitor for my
home office"). The Copilot will collect the asset_type and a written
business justification, then route the request to your reporting
manager for approval. Once approved, IT will assign an available unit
from inventory and notify you for collection or shipment.

Approval flow
-------------
1. Employee submits asset_request with justification.
2. Reporting manager reviews and approves or rejects.
3. If approved, IT verifies inventory availability and assigns the unit.
4. If no inventory is available, IT raises a procurement request which
   may take 2–4 weeks for delivery.

Bring-Your-Own-Device (BYOD)
----------------------------
Personal devices are not permitted to access the corporate network or
business data, with two exceptions: (a) accessing webmail through
Outlook on the Web from a browser, and (b) using Microsoft Authenticator
on a personal mobile phone for MFA. Both require enrolment in Intune.


3. Software Installation Policy
================================

Approved software list
----------------------
The following software is pre-approved and may be installed on demand
through the Software Center application on every company laptop:
- Microsoft 365 suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, OneDrive)
- Visual Studio Code, Visual Studio Community, JetBrains tools
- Git, Node.js, Python (3.11 and 3.12), Docker Desktop
- Slack, Zoom, Webex
- Power BI Desktop, Power Automate Desktop
- Adobe Acrobat Reader (the full Creative Cloud suite is licensed only
  for the Design team)
- Browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Edge

Requesting unlisted software
----------------------------
For any software not in the approved list, raise a ticket with category
"software_install". The request must include: software name and version,
publisher, business justification, and license type (free, trial,
purchased). The IT Security team will review for licensing, vulnerability,
and data-exfiltration risk; review takes up to 3 business days.

Prohibited software
-------------------
The following are explicitly prohibited and will be removed during
quarterly compliance scans:
- Personal cloud storage clients (Dropbox personal, Google Drive personal)
- Torrent / P2P clients (uTorrent, Transmission, qBittorrent)
- Remote access tools not sanctioned by IT (TeamViewer, AnyDesk personal)
- Cracked, pirated, or keygen-patched software of any kind
- Cryptocurrency miners, wallet clients (unless explicitly approved for
  blockchain projects)
- Unsanctioned AI assistant browser extensions that exfiltrate page
  content; only Microsoft Copilot and the internal AI Copilot are
  permitted.

Admin rights
------------
Standard users do NOT have local administrator rights. Temporary admin
elevation (8 hours) can be requested via the IT ticket system with
justification. Permanent admin rights are granted only to IT, DevOps,
and specific engineering roles by exception.


4. VPN and Remote Access
=========================

VPN client
----------
The standard VPN client is Cisco AnyConnect Secure Mobility Client. It
is preinstalled on all company laptops. VPN is required to access:
- Internal Jira, Confluence, GitLab
- Database admin tools and production systems
- Internal file shares
- The internal AI Copilot when working from outside corporate Wi-Fi

Setup steps
-----------
1. Open Cisco AnyConnect from the Start Menu.
2. Connect to: vpn.novigosolutions.com
3. Authenticate with your corporate username and password.
4. Approve the Microsoft Authenticator MFA push when prompted.
5. After connection, the icon turns green and a tray notification
   confirms the tunnel is up.

Work-from-home requirements
---------------------------
Employees working remotely must:
- Use only company-provisioned hardware connected through VPN.
- Ensure home Wi-Fi is WPA2 or WPA3 secured (NOT open networks).
- Lock the screen when stepping away (Win+L on Windows).
- Never connect to the corporate network from public Wi-Fi (cafés,
  airports, hotels) without VPN.

Always-on VPN
-------------
Senior engineers and finance team members may request "always-on" VPN
profiles, which auto-connect on boot using a YubiKey hardware token.
Submit an asset_request with asset_type=vpn_token and category=access.


5. Password and Security Policy
================================

Password rules
--------------
- Minimum 14 characters.
- Must contain upper-case, lower-case, number, and special character.
- Must NOT contain your name, employee ID, or the word "novigo".
- Cannot match any of your last 12 passwords.
- Rotated every 90 days; the system prompts 14 days before expiry.

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
---------------------------------
MFA is mandatory for every corporate account. The default second
factor is Microsoft Authenticator push notification on a personal or
company-provisioned mobile phone. SMS fallback is available but
discouraged. YubiKey is required for users with elevated privileges
(admins, finance, IT).

Account lockout
---------------
Active Directory locks an account after 5 consecutive failed login
attempts. The lockout auto-clears after 30 minutes. To request an
immediate unlock, raise a ticket with category=access; verification
will be done over a video call to confirm identity.

Password resets
---------------
Self-service password reset is available at:
https://passwords.novigosolutions.com from any corporate-managed device.
You will need to verify with MFA. If you are locked out of MFA itself,
contact the IT helpdesk in person or via your manager.

Sharing credentials
-------------------
Sharing your password with anyone — including IT engineers, your
manager, or family members — is a Major Misconduct under the Code of
Conduct and may result in disciplinary action. IT will never ask for
your password.

Phishing and incident reporting
-------------------------------
Forward suspicious emails to phishing@novigosolutions.com and delete the
original. Do NOT click any links, even to "unsubscribe". For any
suspected security incident (lost laptop, leaked credentials, malware
infection), call the IT Security on-call line immediately at
+91-80-IT-ON-CALL.


6. Data and Device Policy
==========================

Disk encryption
---------------
All company laptops are encrypted with BitLocker (Windows) or FileVault
(macOS). The recovery key is stored in Intune and accessible only to
IT administrators.

Personal data on company devices
--------------------------------
Storing personal photos, music libraries, downloaded movies, or
non-work files on company devices is discouraged and may be removed
during routine reimaging. Personal browsing is permitted in moderation
during breaks but is logged.

Data classification
-------------------
- Public: marketing materials, the company website. No restrictions.
- Internal: this handbook, project plans, internal Confluence pages.
  Do not share outside the company.
- Confidential: salary data, HR records, customer PII, source code.
  Encrypt in transit, no copies on personal devices, no email outside
  novigosolutions.com without explicit approval.
- Restricted: financial accounts, security keys, audit reports.
  Access only on a need-to-know basis with logged approval.

Device wipe on exit
-------------------
On termination of employment, IT will remotely wipe and reimage the
laptop on the last working day. Employees must back up any personal
files (within policy) before this point. Refusal to return company
hardware is treated as theft and may be reported to law enforcement.


7. Network and Internet
========================

Wi-Fi networks at office locations
----------------------------------
- CORP-WIFI: standard 2.4 GHz / 5 GHz corporate network. Requires
  domain credentials. Auto-configured on company laptops.
- CORP-WIFI-5G: 5 GHz only, less congested, recommended during peak
  hours (10:00–12:00 IST).
- CORP-IOT: separate VLAN for printers, conference room equipment, and
  smart-board controllers. Not for general use.
- GUEST-WIFI: open network for visitors. Internet only, no internal
  resources, captive portal with daily expiry.

Personal hotspot policy
-----------------------
Tethering to a personal mobile hotspot is allowed only when corporate
Wi-Fi is unavailable AND VPN is connected. Direct internet access via
hotspot without VPN is a policy violation.

Bandwidth-heavy applications
----------------------------
During business hours, please refrain from running large personal
downloads (game updates, streaming Netflix at 4K, BitTorrent of any
kind) on the corporate network. Sustained heavy use is logged and may
be throttled.


8. Hardware Troubleshooting Quick Reference
============================================

Laptop will not boot
--------------------
1. Hold the power button for 15 seconds, release, then press power once.
2. If still nothing, unplug all peripherals and try again.
3. If the screen is black but fans run, try Win+Ctrl+Shift+B (forces
   GPU reset on Windows).
4. If still failing, raise a ticket with category=laptop, priority=high.

Laptop is slow
--------------
- Restart at least once a week. Many slowness issues clear with a
  reboot.
- Check Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc) → Performance tab. If CPU or
  memory is pinned, identify the offending process.
- Run Dell Command Update or HP Support Assistant to install pending
  firmware/driver updates.
- Free up disk space; less than 15% free will degrade performance.
- If the laptop is more than 3 years old, request a refresh under the
  hardware refresh cycle.

External monitor not detected
-----------------------------
1. Check the cable on both ends. USB-C cables in particular are often
   the culprit — try a known-good Thunderbolt 3/4 cable.
2. Win+P to cycle through display modes (PC screen only, Duplicate,
   Extend, Second screen only).
3. Update the dock firmware via Dell Command Update.
4. If on a docking station, unplug the dock from the laptop, wait 10
   seconds, plug back in.

USB device not working
----------------------
- Try a different port. The USB-C ports on most company docks support
  more devices than the USB-A ports.
- Check Device Manager for yellow exclamation marks. Right-click any
  with errors → Uninstall device → reboot. Windows will reinstall.
- For external storage, ensure it is formatted in exFAT or NTFS, not
  HFS+ which is read-only on Windows.


9. Frequently Asked Questions
==============================

Q: How do I get a new laptop / replacement laptop?
A: Use the AI Copilot or raise a ticket. Provide a business
justification. Your manager must approve. Standard refresh is every
3 years; earlier refresh requires hardware failure or special
justification.

Q: Can I use my personal Gmail or WhatsApp on my company laptop?
A: Personal use of webmail and chat is permitted in moderation, but
do not use them for any business communication (you must use Outlook
and Teams for that). Do not enable desktop notifications for personal
accounts as they may leak content during screen sharing.

Q: I am locked out of my account. What do I do?
A: Wait 30 minutes for auto-unlock OR raise a ticket with category=
access for an immediate unlock. The IT engineer will verify your
identity over video call before unlocking.

Q: My MFA app is not sending me notifications.
A: Open Microsoft Authenticator → tap your account → "Check for
notifications". Verify that battery optimisation for Authenticator is
disabled in your phone settings. If still not working, raise a ticket
with category=access.

Q: Can I install Notion / Obsidian / <X> on my laptop?
A: If it is on the approved software list (section 3), yes — install
through Software Center. Otherwise, raise a software_install ticket
with the business justification, and IT Security will review within
3 business days.

Q: How do I request VPN access for a contractor or vendor?
A: Vendor / contractor access is granted only against a signed NDA
and a sponsoring manager. Submit a special-access ticket with the NDA
attached, the sponsoring manager's approval, and the duration of access
required. Maximum default duration is 90 days, renewable.

Q: My laptop was stolen / lost. What do I do?
A: Immediately call the IT Security on-call line (+91-80-IT-ON-CALL)
and email itsec@novigosolutions.com with the time, location, and
circumstances. IT will remotely wipe the device and revoke all session
tokens. File a police FIR and send a copy to HR within 48 hours.

Q: Why was my software install request rejected?
A: Common reasons: the software duplicates an approved tool already
available, has known security vulnerabilities, requires a paid license
not budgeted, or processes sensitive data in non-approved jurisdictions.
The rejection ticket will include the specific reason and may suggest an
approved alternative.

Q: Can I take my laptop on international travel?
A: Yes, with two prior steps: (a) submit a travel notification via the
IT portal at least 5 business days before travel, and (b) for travel
to specific high-risk countries (list maintained by IT Security),
collect a sanitised "travel laptop" from the IT Storeroom instead of
your regular device.

Q: How long are IT tickets retained?
A: Resolved tickets are retained for 3 years for audit and compliance
purposes, then archived. You can view the full history of your own
tickets at any time through the AI Copilot or the self-service portal.
